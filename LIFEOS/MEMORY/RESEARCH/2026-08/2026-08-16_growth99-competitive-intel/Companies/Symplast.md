## Why This Is the Highest-Conviction EMR Bolt-On Target

Compared against the other EMR/clinical players surfaced in this research:
- **Meevo (Millennium Systems International):** minority-backed by Serent Capital since 2021, ~150,000 daily users, processes billions in transactions — far larger scale, would be a major strategic combination rather than a clean tuck-in, and comes with an existing institutional co-owner to negotiate around. Deprioritized as a near-term bolt-on. [MED]
- **Nextech:** owned by TPG, a major PE firm — too large and too expensive to be a "bolt-on"; more realistically a future strategic acquirer of platforms like Growth99 than a target for one. [MED]
- **AestheticsPro, PatientNow:** already-profiled direct competitors with real installed bases (see their own files). Acquiring either would be a much bigger, higher-execution-risk "merger of rivals" deal, not a clean tuck-in, and would command a materially higher price.
- **Symplast**, by contrast, has no competing institutional owner, a specialty-matched product, a plausible size (~$8M ARR range) relative to Growth99's own estimated ~$25–50M revenue, and a single small prior raise suggesting a founder/small-group cap table that is realistic to negotiate directly with. This combination — right specialty, right size, no PE gatekeeper — makes it the standout candidate identified in this pass.
