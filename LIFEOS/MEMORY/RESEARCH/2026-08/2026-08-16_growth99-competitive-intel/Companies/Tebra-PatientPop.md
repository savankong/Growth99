## Overview

- PatientPop, once the leading platform for healthcare practice marketing/growth, merged with Kareo in 2022 to form Tebra. Now offers EHR, billing, online scheduling, reputation management, patient engagement, and healthcare-optimized websites — a much broader (non-aesthetics-specific) practice management suite.
