# EMR / Clinical Charting Technology

**Status:** RESEARCHED
**Category:** Technology — Adjacent Capability Growth99 Lacks
**Value:** HIGH

## Overview

- Electronic medical record (EMR) / electronic health record (EHR) and clinical charting technology — structured treatment charts, before/after photo tools, compliance documentation — is a core capability of competitors **PatientNow** and **AestheticsPro**, and is explicitly and consistently identified by third-party comparison sources as **absent** from Growth99's platform. [MED — consistent across 2 independent competitor-authored comparison pages, but not yet confirmed by a neutral source] — [PatientNow comparison](https://www.patientnow.com/resources/compare/best-medical-spa-crm), [AestheticsPro vs. Vagaro](https://www.aestheticspro.com/Blog/AestheticsPro-Vs.-Vagaro/)
- Strategic significance: this gap is the technical boundary of Growth99's addressable market — practices that want a single clinical + marketing system will look elsewhere unless Growth99 integrates with or acquires EMR/EHR capability. Directly relevant to the diligence question on adjacent-vertical (dental/general medical) expansion, since clinical documentation requirements differ meaningfully by vertical.
- **Pass 4 note:** the exit-readiness plan ([PE-ACQUISITION-THESIS.md](../PE-ACQUISITION-THESIS.md)) addresses this gap through a lighter-weight partnership/integration approach rather than acquisition in the near term — see that document's "Path Forward Without a Near-Term Clinical Software Acquisition" section. The gap itself remains real and unresolved technically; only the near-term remediation strategy has changed.
