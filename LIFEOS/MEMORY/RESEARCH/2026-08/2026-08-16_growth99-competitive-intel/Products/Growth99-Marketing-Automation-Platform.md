# Growth99 Marketing Automation Platform (Core)

**Status:** RESEARCHED
**Category:** Product — Core Platform
**Value:** CRITICAL

## Overview

- Described by Growth99 as an "integrated marketing automation platform" combining CRM, review/reputation management, lead generation, client communications, SEO, paid advertising, and content marketing into a single system. [HIGH] — [Growth99 — Home](https://growth99.com/), [About Us](https://growth99.com/about-us/)
- Sold both as **software** (the platform itself) and as **managed services** (Growth99 staff executing SEO/ad campaigns on the practice's behalf) — this dual model is central to the "revenue mix / services vs. SaaS" diligence question, since Searcher Insights labels the business "pure B2B SaaS" while Growth99's own marketing pages describe hands-on service delivery. [MED, internally conflicting across sources] — [Growth99 — Marketing Your Practice](https://growth99.com/solutions/marketing-your-practice/) vs. [Searcher Insights](https://searcherinsights.com/network/deals/growth99/)
- A third-party comparison source (PatientNow, itself a competitor) characterizes Growth99 as strong on "the front end of the patient journey" (website, SEO, ads, reputation, CRM) but placing "less emphasis on scheduling, clinical records, or day-to-day operations." [MED, competitor-authored] — [PatientNow comparison](https://www.patientnow.com/resources/compare/best-medical-spa-crm)
- No independent EMR/clinical-charting capability found — consistent across multiple competitor comparison pages (see Companies/PatientNow.md, Companies/AestheticsPro.md). This is a structural product gap relative to clinically-integrated competitors, and matters for both the "proprietary IP" and "adjacent vertical expansion" diligence questions.

## Open Questions for Next Pass

- How much of the platform (SEO/ad tooling, review management, CRM) is Growth99-built vs. licensed/white-labeled third-party software — not disclosed publicly.
- Real usage data (feature adoption, manual-vs-automated delivery ratio) would require management access; flagged for direct diligence rather than fabricated.
