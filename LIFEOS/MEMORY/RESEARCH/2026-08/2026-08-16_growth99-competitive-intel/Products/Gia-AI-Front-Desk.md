# Gia (AI Front Desk)

**Status:** RESEARCHED
**Category:** Product — Growth99's flagship AI feature
**Value:** CRITICAL

## Overview

- AI-powered lead-response engine that engages inbound inquiries across website forms, Facebook Messenger, Instagram DMs, SMS text, and CallRail-tracked calls — "simultaneously, automatically, and from day one," responding within 60 seconds, 24/7. [HIGH] — [Growth99 — Gia product page](https://growth99.com/products/gia-ai-front-desk-for-aesthetic-practices/), [Growth99 — AI info page](https://growth99.com/ai-info-page/)
- Trained during setup on the practice's brand voice, services, and website; described as "learning from feedback over time" to refine responses. [HIGH] — same sources
- Human handoff built in: escalates medical questions, complex situations, or direct requests to speak with staff. [HIGH] — same sources
- Covered in trade press: American Med Spa Association and National Law Review both ran the Growth99 announcement of Gia as "an AI front desk built to convert new patient inquiries across every channel." [MED — press pickups, largely restating Growth99's own framing] — [AmSpa](https://www.americanmedspa.org/news/growth99-introduces-gia-an-ai-front-desk-built-to-convert-new-patient-inquiries-across-every-channel/), [National Law Review](https://natlawreview.com/press-releases/growth99-introduces-gia-ai-front-desk-built-convert-new-patient-inquiries)
